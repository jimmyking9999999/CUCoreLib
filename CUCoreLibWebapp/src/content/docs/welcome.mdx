## What is CUCoreLib?

CUCoreLib is a BepInEx + Harmony library mod for Casualties: Unknown. Other mods depend on it to register content and reuse small runtime helpers, instead of each mod patching the game on its own.

Adding a single custom item by hand means writing patches for the item list, sprites, loot pools, traders, saving, and recipes. With CUCoreLib that is one `ItemRegistry.Register` call, so your mod can focus on the fun part: designing new items, recipes, and assets.
